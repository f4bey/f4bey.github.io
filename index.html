01 / About

Between the shop floor, IT and the business case.


I've spent my career at the intersection of manufacturing, technology and commercial management. At Bosch, I own digital products for the global manufacturing network – currently a global Energy Management System that gives plants transparency on energy consumption from plant level down to individual machines.

Before that I worked in procurement and partner management at Porsche, which taught me how to negotiate, structure vendor relationships and keep a business case honest. I hold a Master of Engineering in Smart Factory – Industry 4.0.

What I enjoy most: taking a complex, multi-site transformation, finding the simplest product that solves the real problem, and getting people to actually use it.
