About

Between the shop floor, IT and the business case.

I'm a manufacturing IT product leader with experience scaling industrial technology across manufacturing sites worldwide. I combine product strategy, technology transformation and commercial expertise with cross-functional leadership.

At Bosch, I own a global manufacturing operations platform for energy management – used by production and facility management teams in plants across three continents. I built its product and operating model and led the rollout. Before that, I led strategic sourcing for the Volkswagen Group's global connectivity ecosystem at Porsche, delivering one of the largest cost savings in Volkswagen Group sourcing.

What I enjoy most: translating business requirements into scalable technology solutions – and making them work across IT, operations and engineering.
